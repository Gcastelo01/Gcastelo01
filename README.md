# Gabriel Castelo Branco

**Artificial Intelligence Research · Software, Data, and Infrastructure Engineering**  
B.Sc. in Computer Science from **UFMG** · Belo Horizonte, Brazil

I am a researcher and software developer working at the intersection of **AI, software engineering, and data systems**, with a strong interest in understanding systems end to end: from models and algorithms to the infrastructure, networks, and services that keep them running.

I enjoy combining technical investigation, experimentation, and implementation. For me, it is not enough to make something work — it also matters to understand **why it works, how to evaluate its limits, and how to make it reproducible and reliable**.

## What I work on and research

- **Artificial Intelligence:** applied research at **FutureLab / UFMG**, involving machine learning, foundation models, intelligent agents, and digital twins.
- **Software and data engineering:** backend development, APIs, data pipelines, and analytical systems, with attention to architecture, testing, and operations.
- **Language models:** experiments with open-weight LLMs, local inference, quantization, model evaluation, and agent-based workflows.
- **Networking and infrastructure:** Linux environments, virtualization, containers, service connectivity, reverse proxies, DNS, security, and observability.

## Networks, systems, and personal lab

Beyond research and development projects, I explore infrastructure through my own *homelab* environment. This is where I experiment with **Proxmox, Linux virtual machines, Docker/Podman, HAProxy, ZeroTier, local DNS, firewalls, and self-hosted services**.

I am especially interested in the relationship between **architecture, performance, security, and reliability**, including the infrastructure required to run AI models locally.
